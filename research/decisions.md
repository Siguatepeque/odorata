# Current recommendation and open items

Start here in a new session. This is a working recommendation, not a conclusion: it changes whenever a dated re-read contradicts it ([[README]]). Written 2026-11-23, Black Friday week, from single observations.

## Question: is it meaningful to buy complete lines as a reseller?

**No, not for the first purchase.** Buy the few bottles that earn margin and sell, then widen. Reasons, from the data:

1. **Demand falls with every flanker.** Fragrantica votes for ADG: original 26,338, Profondo 12,248, Parfum 7,632, EDP 4,012 (see [[candidates]]). The original brings the customers; the flankers sell to people who already know it.
2. **Part of a line fails the margin gate.** ADG Parfum 100 ml (ratio 1.39) and Elixir 50 ml (1.45) don't clear 1.6, because Privé prices them close to the US price. Carrying them ties up about L3,400–3,900 each for roughly break-even.
3. **Capital.** One of each passing or exclusive ADG variant is about L22,000 landed, versus about L2,000 for the EDT alone. The original plan's whole launch shelf was about L21,600 across nine bottles ([[pricing-filter]]). Spending it on one brand concentrates risk.
4. **There are no supplier terms.** You buy retail from Jomashop with coupons, not wholesale. Breadth gives no price advantage; it is only worth it if customers ask for the range, which is unproven.
5. **Coupon risk scales with breadth.** Only ADG EDT and ADG EDP 75 ml keep a clear margin without Jomashop's 20% coupon; the Profondo variants don't ([[signals]]). More variants means more exposure to one coupon.

**What would change this:** evidence that customers want the range (messages asking for other ADG variants), or a wholesale account that cuts buy prices below Jomashop's coupon price ([[discounters]]).

## First purchase = the test import (revised 2026-11-23)

Buy ADG EDT 100 and ADG EDP 75 together as the 2-bottle test import, once GBox confirms it takes perfume, ideally while the Black Friday coupon runs. About L4,080 landed. They are the only bottles that keep a margin if the coupon goes (about 23–24%) or if customs charges (about 19–21%), so the test carries little downside. Losing both at once leaves them about break-even ([[signals]]). The one shipment answers A3 (customs), A4 (GBox), A10 (real weight and freight) and gives a first sale against Privé.
