# Three signals, kept separate

Margin is the gate; demand ranks what passes; the Privé gap is context. No combined score ([[README]]). Read 2026-11-23, Black Friday week, single observations ([[assumptions]]).

- **Margin** = Privé ÷ (Jomashop price × L26.8), at the coupon price and without the coupon. Source: [[discounters]], `data/prive-2026-11-23.csv`.
- **Demand** = Fragrantica votes (lifetime, so it favors old scents) and rating.
- **Gap** = state at Privé from the CSV.

## The coupon stress test (A2)

Every ratio above 1.6 depends on Jomashop's 20% coupon. Without it:

| Bottle | Ratio with coupon | Ratio without | Margin at Privé −10%, with → without coupon |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADG EDT 100 ml | 2.28 | 1.82 | about 36% → 23% |
| Most Wanted Parfum | 1.85 | 1.48 | about → 6% |
| Stronger With You Intensely | 1.78 | 1.43 | about → 5% |
| Eros EDP | 1.84 | 1.47 | about → 5% |
| ADG Profondo EDP | 1.75 | 1.40 | fails |
| La Vie Est Belle | 2.02 | about 1.62 (assumes standard 20%) | marginal |
| Good Girl | 1.73 | about 1.39 (assumes standard 20%) | fails |

Margins use landed cost = price × 1.07 + US$5, card 4.25% + US$0.35, L120 delivery, L25 packaging. Only ADG EDT stays clearly profitable without the coupon. Coupon durability could not be confirmed: third-party coupon pages are inconsistent and some of the 20% offers are code-based.

## Louis Vuitton Imagination

Strong demand (4.51, 14,809 votes) and no Privé listing, but no verified discounted source: LV sells the 100 ml itself, and cheaper copies are eBay and marketplace listings with counterfeit complaints. Not a safe buy until a legitimate source is found.
