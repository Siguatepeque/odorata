# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router). Motion via GSAP. Commerce backend will be Shopify (catalog, inventory, checkout); this build is a front-end proof of concept with mock data shaped so Shopify can replace it.

## Users

Honduran shoppers, mostly on phones, often arriving from Instagram or WhatsApp. Many are less familiar with ecommerce and wary of paying online: they need to know exactly what they get, what it costs in Lempiras, how they pay, and when and how it arrives. Two jobs: repurchase a fragrance they already know, or find a new one by what it smells like and when they'd wear it.

## Product Purpose

A curated multi-brand fragrance store for Honduras. It must feel like a thoughtfully curated boutique (Dover Street Market, The Conran Shop, Voo Store in spirit) while working with the clarity of an everyday supermarket: obvious navigation, recognizable buttons, visible boundaries, plain product information, total price shown up front, and a short checkout. Success: a first-time visitor goes from landing to a confirmed order on mobile without hesitation.

## Positioning

A curator, not a brand: the store frames many houses (designer, niche, and "inspired by" alternatives) and explains them plainly. Its personality lives in the selection and the art direction, never in ambiguity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Front-end mock: home, collection, product page, bag, multi-step checkout UI (contact, delivery, payment choice, review, confirmation). No real payments or orders; Shopify connects later.
- Spanish (Honduras) is the default: the site always opens in /es. English is available from the footer. Prices in Lempiras (L).
- Payment options shown should match local habits (cash on delivery, bank transfer, card); delivery explained in plain terms.
- Mobile-first, accessible (keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion), performant.

## Brand Commitments

- Retailer name: **Odorata**.
- Products, houses, and prices in the mock are illustrative placeholders, to be replaced by Shopify data.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No product photography, reviews, ratings, press, or customer counts exist. Do not fabricate testimonials, ratings, awards, or "as seen in" claims. Authenticity and delivery promises must stay generic until the owner confirms them.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity before charm: every editorial gesture leaves the path to buying obvious.
2. No surprises: price, size, concentration, delivery, and payment are explicit before the bag.
3. Describe, don't hype: what it smells like, when to wear it, how long it lasts, in plain words.
4. Motion carries meaning: animation clarifies state and continuity, or it does not ship.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Bag and checkout fully operable by keyboard with announced state changes. Large tap targets; labels never replaced by icons alone.
